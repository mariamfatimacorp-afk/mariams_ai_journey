Welcome

How to use this pack

  • 🖨️ Print any page, any time — you can print every page as many times as you like for your own family.
  • ⭐ Start with the certificate — fill in your child's name after their first StoryMagic story and watch their face light up.
  • 🎨 Coloring pages pair with the stories: read the story first, then color its hero together.
  • 🌙 Urdu word cards — cut along the dashed lines. Show the card while you read stories in Urdu mode.
  • 🛏️ Bedtime chart & reward chart — stick them on the fridge; one star sticker per calm bedtime works wonders.
