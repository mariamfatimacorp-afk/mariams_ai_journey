# 🎬 KidsTube Studio — Business #2

**The "sell shovels" business:** tools for the thousands of people trying to start kids' YouTube channels. Creators pay far more readily than viewers — and every customer is also proof for your own channel expertise.

**What it does:** pick a niche + type a topic → get a complete video kit in one click: song lyrics (with Suno-ready `[Verse]/[Chorus]` structure), an AI-music style prompt, 3 title options, an SEO description, tags, thumbnail text ideas, and a pre-upload checklist (including the legally-required "Made for Kids" setting).

**Niches covered:** nursery rhymes, lullabies, ABC/phonics, Islamic kids songs, and bilingual Urdu–English — the last two are underserved and are where your own channel advantage lives.

## Business model
| Tier | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Free tool | unlimited kits, no signup | $0 — this is the lead magnet |
| **Pro Kit** | 30-day content calendar, 50 tested Suno prompts, branding worksheet, thumbnail templates, "first 1,000 subs" playbook | **$19** (Gumroad, PDF/Notion bundle) |
| Later | "done-for-you channel setup" service | $99–199 per client |

## Launch checklist
1. Live automatically with GitHub Pages (same setup as StoryMagic):
   `https://<username>.github.io/mariams_ai_journey/apps/kidstube-studio/`
2. Build the **Pro Kit** contents (calendar + prompt library + playbook — can be generated next session) and list on Gumroad at $19.
3. Set `BUY_URL` in `index.html` to the Gumroad link.
4. **Distribution — this decides everything.** Post where kids-channel creators gather:
   - Reddit: r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube ("I made a free tool that plans a kids' song video in 30 seconds")
   - Facebook groups: "Cocomelon style creators", "Faceless YouTube channels", "YouTube Automation"
   - Your own YouTube: "I built a free tool for kids' channel creators" — creators searching "how to make nursery rhyme videos with AI" find you
5. Watch which niche gets used most (add Google Analytics later) — that's your Pro Kit's best audience.
